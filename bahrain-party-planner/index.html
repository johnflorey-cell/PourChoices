How it works

  1. Enter your guests and hours, or type the drinks you want: a type like lager or prosecco, or a brand like Heineken or Ballantine's.
  2. Choose Value, Mid or Premium, or set a budget.
  3. Get one shopping list per shop, with the total and delivery, and open each shop to order.

It works for a weekly or monthly shop too, and tells you when your saved plan gets cheaper.

Questions

How much alcohol do I need for a party?

Allow 1 drink per guest per hour if they drink lightly, 2 for an average crowd and 2.75 if they drink generously, then add 20% spare so you never run short. A can of beer is one drink, a 75cl bottle of wine is about 5 glasses and a 75cl bottle of spirits is about 16 single measures.

Where is the cheapest place to buy drinks for a party in Bahrain?

It depends on what you buy. The Bahrain Party Planner checks BMMI, African & Eastern, GBI Express and NHSC, then splits your list between shops only when that saves money after delivery charges and minimum orders.

Can I plan to a budget?

Yes. Enter your budget and what you'd like. If it costs more, the planner first picks cheaper drinks so you keep your quantities, and only cuts quantities if it still doesn't fit.
